WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

Our Story Since 2012

UncleM's Foodtruck started as a small local family business in 2012. Its original purpose was to provide people with fast, convenient food while contributing to the local economy.

In the beginning, the business faced challenges as it worked to establish itself. However, through dedication, hard work and continued growth, it gradually developed into one of the recognised local food stops in the Soshanguve Block W community.

Today, our goal is to continue growing, improving our services and making quality food accessible to more customers.
